On the green field the players stand,
Bat and ball in eager hand.
The bowler runs with fiery pace,
The batsman waits to find his space.

Leather meets willow, the crowd takes flight,
A six soars high, a glorious sight.
The fielders chase with hearts so fast,
Hoping the ball won’t sail past.

A dot, a single, pressure grows,
Every ball a story shows.
The keeper dives, the stumps are near,
The batsman’s breath is caught in fear.

Cheers erupt, the wicket falls,
Victory whispers, destiny calls.
Yet till the final run is made,
Hope and tension never fade.

Cricket’s not just a simple game,
It’s passion, pride, and timeless flame.
